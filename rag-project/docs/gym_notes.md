# Gym notes

## Leg day
Squats 4x6 at 80kg, Romanian deadlifts (RDL) 3x8 at 70kg, walking lunges 3x12 per leg.
Finish with leg press 3x15 and standing calf raises 4x15.

Keep squats below parallel. If the lower back rounds on RDLs, drop the weight by 10kg.

## Push day
Bench press 4x6 at 70kg, overhead press 3x8 at 40kg, incline dumbbell press 3x10.
Finish with dips 3x10 and tricep pushdowns 3x12.

## Pull day
Deadlifts 3x5 at 110kg, pull-ups 4x8, barbell rows 3x8 at 60kg.
Finish with face pulls 3x15 and bicep curls 3x12.

## Progressive overload
When I hit all reps for two sessions in a row, add 2.5kg to the bar.
For dumbbell exercises, add reps first (up to 12), then move up 2kg.

## Rest and recovery
Rest 2-3 minutes between heavy compound sets and 60-90 seconds on accessories.
Aim for 7-8 hours of sleep. Take a deload week every 6-8 weeks at about 60% of normal weight.

## Nutrition
Protein target is roughly 1.6g per kg of bodyweight per day.
Pre-workout: banana and coffee about 45 minutes before training.
Post-workout: a proper meal with protein and rice within 2 hours.
