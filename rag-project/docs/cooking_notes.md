# Cooking notes

## Dal tadka
Wash 1 cup toor dal and pressure cook with 3 cups water, turmeric and salt for 3 whistles.
For the tadka, heat ghee, add jeera, garlic, dried red chilli and a pinch of hing.
Pour the tadka over the dal and finish with coriander.

## Chicken curry
Marinate 500g chicken in curd, chilli powder, turmeric and salt for 30 minutes.
Fry onions until deep brown, as this is where most of the flavour comes from.
Add ginger-garlic paste and tomatoes, cook until oil separates, then add the chicken.
Simmer covered for 20-25 minutes.

## Egg bhurji
Scramble 4 eggs with onion, green chilli, tomato and a pinch of pav bhaji masala.
Keep the heat medium so the eggs stay soft, not rubbery.
Quick dinner in under 10 minutes.

## High-protein meal prep
Cook chicken, rice and dal in bulk on Sunday for the week's lunches.
Paneer bhurji and boiled eggs make easy protein-heavy snacks.
Store portions in airtight containers; they keep for about 4 days in the fridge.

## Kitchen tips
Soak rajma and chole overnight, or they won't soften properly.
Rinse basmati rice until the water runs clear for fluffier rice.
Add salt to dal after cooking if it's taking too long to soften.
